**Title:** Murmur: review pull requests by talking | No Hands: The Wispr Challenge

**Visibility:** Unlisted

**Description:**
Murmur turns a spoken code review into precise inline comments on the exact lines of a GitHub pull request. Paste a PR, talk it through with Wispr Flow, and every comment lands on a real line with the sentence you said attached.

What makes it different:
1. Your sentence is the receipt: every comment carries the words that made it.
2. It can't invent a line: every anchor is checked against the real diff.
3. It says only what you said: no opinions of its own.
4. It just works: live, no sign-in, nothing stored.

Live: https://murmur-voice-review.netlify.app
Code: https://github.com/jenish1345/murmur

Built for No Hands: The Wispr Challenge (WeMakeDevs x Wispr Flow), Builders track. #NoHandsChallenge
Narration is synthesized. The app footage is a real recording of the live site.
